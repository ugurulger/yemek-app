## Description
```
Never wonder what's for dinner again.

MyKitchen turns meal planning from a weekly chore into a two-minute habit. Pick your meals, and your grocery list writes itself.

PLAN YOUR WEEK IN MINUTES
Drag your favorite meals onto a simple weekly plan. Breakfast, lunch, dinner — as much or as little planning as you like.

YOUR RECIPES, FINALLY ORGANIZED
Save family favorites and new discoveries in one place. Find any recipe in seconds when it's time to cook.

FROM PLAN TO SHOPPING LIST, AUTOMATICALLY
Your weekly plan becomes a tidy grocery list — grouped, checkable, and ready for the store. No more forgotten ingredients.

COOK WITH LESS STRESS
Open today's plan and start cooking. No decision fatigue, no 5 PM panic, no "let's just order in… again."

MyKitchen is free to use. Made for busy people who still want good food on the table.

Questions or ideas? We'd love to hear from you: uurefe@gmail.com
```
