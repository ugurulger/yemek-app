## Frequently asked questions

**How do I add items to my kitchen?**
On the *My Kitchen* tab, scan your fridge with the camera, upload a receipt or photo, or use *Add with assistant* to type or dictate a list.

**Where do the recipes come from?**
Recipes are generated for *your* ingredients. Pick your preferences on the Recipes tab and refresh whenever your kitchen changes.

**How does the grocery list work?**
Missing ingredients from recipes you select are collected on the *Groceries* tab, grouped by category, with price comparison for Albert Heijn and Jumbo.

**Is my data private?**
Yes — your inventory, recipes and plans stay on your device. See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html) for details.

**How do I delete my data?**
Deleting the app deletes all of your content. For analytics deletion requests, email us.
