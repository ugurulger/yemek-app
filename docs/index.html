Support

Questions? We read every message.

We're a small team and usually reply within a few days. Email us at uurefe@gmail.com.

How do I add items to my kitchen?

On the My Kitchen tab, scan your fridge with the camera, upload a receipt or photo, or type or dictate a list with the assistant.

Where do the recipes come from?

Recipes are generated for your ingredients. Pick your preferences on the Recipes tab and refresh whenever your kitchen changes.

How does the grocery list work?

Missing ingredients from recipes you select are collected on the Groceries tab, grouped by category, with price comparison for Albert Heijn and Jumbo.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your inventory, recipes and plans stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app deletes all of your content. For analytics deletion requests, email us at uurefe@gmail.com.
